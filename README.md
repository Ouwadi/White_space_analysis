# White_space_analysis

This project aims to explore the relationship between job density across districts and associated Service Level Agreement (SLA) performance pressures. The analysis focuses on jobs carried out between October 2024 and March 2025, using SLA pass rates calculated without the use of exclusion codes to ensure a true reflection of performance.

Initial observations highlight a high volume of jobs across several regions. A key challenge identified is the misalignment between sales team regions and operational planning regions, which has contributed to delays in early-stage job planning. These delays are exacerbated by limited resource availability, impacting our ability to meet SLA targets efficiently.

The insights from this analysis will support better resource planning and alignment strategies to improve SLA performance in high-demand areas.
